# PROMPTS

## AI Summary Prompt

Generate a professional 100-word summary for an AI spend audit report.

The summary should:
- Explain where the company is overspending
- Mention optimization opportunities
- Mention estimated monthly and annual savings
- Sound professional and actionable
- Avoid exaggerated claims

Input:
- Tool list
- Current spending
- Savings opportunities
- Team size
- Primary use case

Output:
- A concise audit summary paragraph

---

## Why I Designed It This Way

The prompt focuses on clarity, professionalism, and actionable recommendations. The goal is to generate summaries useful for startup founders and engineering managers.

---

## What Didn’t Work

Initially, broader prompts generated generic and repetitive responses. Narrowing the prompt to focus specifically on spend optimization and recommendations improved output quality significantly.
