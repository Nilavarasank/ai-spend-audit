# Metrics

## North Star Metric

Completed audit reports per week.

This metric directly measures how many users receive value from the product and enter the lead generation funnel.

---

## Input Metrics

### 1. Landing Page Conversion Rate
Percentage of visitors who complete an audit.

### 2. Lead Capture Rate
Percentage of users who submit email/company details after seeing results.

### 3. Consultation Request Rate
Percentage of high-savings users who request Credex consultation.

---

## Initial Instrumentation

The first analytics I would track:
- Page visits
- Audit completions
- Savings generated
- Lead submissions
- Consultation clicks

---

## Pivot Trigger

If audit completion rate stays below 10% after significant traffic, the onboarding flow or value proposition likely needs redesign.

If lead capture remains below 5%, the post-audit experience or perceived value may be too weak.
