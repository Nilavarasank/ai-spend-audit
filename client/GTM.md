# Go To Market Strategy

## Target User

The primary target user is a startup founder, engineering manager, or technical lead at a small-to-medium startup with 5–50 employees actively using AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, and GitHub Copilot.

---

## User Behavior

These users typically search for:
- “Best AI coding tools”
- “Reduce AI API costs”
- “ChatGPT Team vs Enterprise”
- “AI tools for startups”

They also browse:
- Hacker News
- Reddit
- Indie Hackers
- Twitter/X
- Product Hunt

---

## Communities

Potential users spend time in:
- r/startups
- r/ChatGPT
- Indie Hackers
- AI engineering Discord communities
- SaaS founder Slack groups

---

## First 100 Users Strategy

1. Share the tool on Reddit startup communities.
2. Post a Product Hunt launch.
3. Share screenshots and savings examples on Twitter/X.
4. Ask developer communities for feedback.
5. Reach out to startup founders directly through LinkedIn and Twitter.
