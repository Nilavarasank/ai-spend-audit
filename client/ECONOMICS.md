# Economics

## Lead Value Estimate

If a startup converts through Credex and purchases discounted AI credits, the estimated average value per converted customer could range from $500–$3000 annually depending on usage volume.

Assuming an average converted customer value of $1500/year and a 20% margin, each converted customer could generate approximately $300 in profit.

---

## Customer Acquisition Cost

Estimated CAC by channel:

- Reddit / Community Marketing: $5–$15
- Product Hunt launch: $20–$40
- Twitter/X organic: Near $0
- Founder outreach: Time-intensive but low direct cost

Average blended CAC estimate: $25.

---

## Conversion Funnel

Estimated funnel:

- Audit completed → Consultation booked: 10%
- Consultation booked → Paid customer: 20%

This means:
1000 audits
→ 100 consultations
→ 20 customers

At $300 profit/customer:
20 × $300 = $6000 profit

## Path to $1M ARR

To achieve $1M ARR:

- Need approximately 3333 customers paying average $300/year profit contribution
- Requires strong organic distribution and recurring inbound startup traffic
- Product Hunt, SEO, founder communities, and partnerships could drive sustainable growth
