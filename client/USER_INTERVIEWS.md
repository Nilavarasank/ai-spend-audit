# User Interviews

## Interview 1

### Person
R.K — CS student building AI side projects

### Quotes
- “I pay for multiple AI tools and honestly forget which ones I even use.”
- “I would definitely use something that shows cheaper alternatives.”
- “Most people don’t track AI subscriptions carefully.”

### Surprising Insight
He cared more about simplifying subscriptions than just saving money.

### Design Changes
I focused more on clarity and recommendation explanations.

## Interview 2

### Person
S.M — Small startup founder

### Quotes
- “AI costs increased faster than expected this year.”
- “We upgraded plans too early for our team size.”
- “An audit tool would help during budgeting.”

### Surprising Insight
They mainly wanted confidence that their spending decisions were correct.

### Design Changes
Added more detailed reasoning behind recommendations.

## Interview 3

### Person
A.P — Freelance developer

### Quotes
- “I switch between ChatGPT, Claude, and Cursor constantly.”
- “I forget recurring subscriptions.”
- “A savings dashboard would be useful.”

### Surprising Insight
Tool overlap was a bigger problem than pricing alone.

### Design Changes
Improved multi-tool input workflow and dashboard visibility.
